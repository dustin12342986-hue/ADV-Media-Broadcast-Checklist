What it is

Blue Bonnet is an assistant built into the ADV Media apps. You type a question the way you'd ask a person, and it answers using what it's been told about how we run our shows.

Think of it as the thing you'd normally text Dustin or Aracely about at 4pm on a Friday — call times, where to check in, how the checklist works, what a piece of gear does. It's there so you don't have to wait on a reply.

Where to find it

🌼

Bottom right corner of the app.

A round bluebonnet button. Tap it, type your question, hit send.

It sits on top of whatever screen you're on and closes again when you're done. Nothing you do in it changes anything in the app.

What to ask it

Plain questions work best. It's not a search box — write it the way you'd say it.

  • What time is call time for the next game?
  • Where do I check in?
  • How do I use the broadcast checklist?
  • What positions still need filling?
  • What's the difference between Assist and Show Assist?
  • How do I clean a SMPTE fiber connector?
  • I have no camera control but the picture is fine — what do I check?

If the first answer isn't quite it, say so and add detail. "No, I meant the Thursday game" works fine — it follows the conversation.

What it knows

AreaWhat's covered
The season Home teams, call times, where and with whom to check in, bag policy.
Positions What each crew position does and how the control room fits together.
The apps Clocking in, the pre and post shift checklists, Signal Up, Request Help, submitting your invoice.
Audio gear The SQ-5 console and ULX-D wireless — I/O, gain, setup order, IR sync, and the faults that look broken but aren't.
Camera & fiber SMPTE cleaning and troubleshooting, URSA Broadcast, the fiber converters, and camera control.

Each app has its own version. The one in the crew app knows about broadcast; it doesn't know about the other contracts, and they don't know about ours.

What it can't do

It answers questions. It doesn't press buttons. Blue Bonnet cannot:

  • Clock you in or out
  • Pick up, drop, or assign a shift
  • Submit or change an invoice
  • See your profile, your pay, or anyone's personal details
  • Change anything on the schedule or the run of show

If it tells you how to do one of those, it's telling you where the button is — you still have to go tap it.

Don't paste personal information into it. No bank details, no Social Security number, no medical information, no anyone else's phone number. Your messages go out to an AI service to be answered. Direct deposit info goes by email to the office, never here.

How much to trust it

It's useful and it's usually right. It is not the source of truth, and it can be confidently wrong.

For thisTrust
Your call timeThe Today tab in the app. That's live.
Who's working / open shiftsThe Shifts tab. That's live.
Tonight's cuesThe Run of Show tab.
How gear worksBlue Bonnet is a good start. The manual settles it.
Anything about your payAsk the office. Not the assistant.
Don't troubleshoot a live show through it. If something breaks on air, get on comms and tell your lead. Ask Blue Bonnet afterwards, or before doors, when being wrong costs nothing.

And if an answer contradicts what Dustin, Aracely, or your lead told you — they're right. Tell us, so we can fix what it's been taught.
