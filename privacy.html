This Privacy Policy explains what information ADV Media Teams (the "App") collects, how it is used, and who can see it. It applies to the App and any branded or customized version of it deployed for a client organization.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and profile photo, via Google Sign-In.
  • Profile details you provide: crew handle, bio, skills, phone number.
  • Location data: your device's GPS coordinates at the moment you clock in for a shift, used to confirm you are at an authorized worksite.
  • Work records: clock-in/out times, position assignments, checklist completions, shift pickups/drops.
  • Financial information: hours worked, pay rate, invoice totals, and notes you submit for payment.

2. How We Use Information

  • To verify identity and worksite presence at clock-in.
  • To display your digital ID card and crew profile to authorized personnel and venue staff.
  • To process and approve invoices, including sharing relevant details with accounting systems such as QuickBooks Online.
  • To allow designated administrators to manage scheduling, shift coverage, and payment approval.

3. Who Can See Your Information

Most personal and financial data (including your phone number, invoice history, and precise clock-in location) is restricted to designated administrators of your organization. Your name, photo, skills, and current shift assignment may be visible to other crew members using the App, since coordination requires it. Location data specifically is never visible to other crew members, only administrators.

4. Data Retention

Clock-in/out records and invoice submissions are kept in a permanent record for accounting, audit, and dispute-resolution purposes, even if a corresponding entry is later removed from your own personal view within the App. This permanent record is accessible only to designated administrators.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google — used for account sign-in. Google's own privacy policy applies to that sign-in process.
  • QuickBooks Online (Intuit) — used by administrators to process approved invoice payments. Intuit's own privacy policy applies to data once it is transmitted to QuickBooks.
  • Formspree — used to deliver invoice and approval notification emails.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including authenticated access controls, to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted through the App.

7. Location Data — Additional Notice

Location is captured only at the moment of clock-in, not continuously and not in the background. You may decline location permission on your device, though this may prevent you from clocking in depending on how your organization has configured the App. Some states have specific legal requirements for employee/contractor location tracking; the organization deploying the App to its own crew is responsible for providing any legally required notice or obtaining consent beyond what this Policy provides.

8. Your Choices

  • You may decline location permission at any time through your device settings.
  • You may request deletion of your profile information by contacting your organization's administrator, subject to Section 4 above regarding permanent work and payment records.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
