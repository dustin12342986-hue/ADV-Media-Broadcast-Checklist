A Note from Aracely, Founder & CEO

Everything we do at ADV Media comes back to one thing: how we make our clients feel. They need to feel heard. They need to feel like they can trust us — through the way we dress, the way we carry ourselves, and the way we show up in their spaces. That kind of professionalism isn't something you can fake and it isn't something we can manufacture for you. It has to come from within.

What we can do is outline it. Not as a rulebook, but as a guide for your own personal success and for the success of this team. When you win, ADV wins. When ADV wins, we all eat. This is what that looks like in practice.

Welcome to the crew. We are so glad you are here.

— Aracely

How We Show Up On Site

Arrival

We ask that you arrive at your call time, not show time, but call time. That buffer exists for a reason and it gives everyone, including you, a chance to settle in and feel prepared before the work begins. Before you head to your position, check in through the ADV Media Teams app and confirm your digital ID card is active. It also helps to review the advance beforehand so you already have a feel for the space and what the day looks like.

Appearance

Dress code is communicated per event through the ADV Media Teams platform so you always know what is expected ahead of time. A few things stay consistent across every event:

  • Your ADV Media shirt is part of your gear, just like your lanyard and media pass. One shirt is provided to every contractor at no cost when you join the team. It should be clean and wrinkle free every time you wear it: treat it like the credential it is.
  • Your media pass and lanyard are required on every site, every time. No exceptions. They identify you as part of the team and make it easier for venue staff, clients, and campus personnel to recognize that you belong there.
  • Closed-toe shoes are required on every set and every stage. This is a safety standard as much as a dress standard.
  • Shorts and skirts must fall no shorter than knuckle length when your arms are relaxed at your sides.
  • Everything you wear should fit well and stay in place, whether you are squatting to run cable, reaching overhead, or working outside in the wind — your clothing should move with you without becoming a distraction or a hazard.
  • Flowy or very loose clothing tends to catch on equipment and get in the way of the work; fitted and functional is always the right call.

We want you to feel like yourself on site. These guidelines exist to protect you and to make sure the client sees a team they can trust.

On Site

Production environments can get busy and a little chaotic, and that is completely normal. A few things that help everything run smoothly:

  • Stay present and on your post during the show. Your position matters and the team counts on you being where you are supposed to be.
  • If something unexpected comes up, handle it calmly and quietly; clients and audiences take their cues from the crew, and a calm crew makes everyone feel safe.
  • If a client or venue staff member approaches you with a question outside your area, a warm "let me connect you with the right person" goes a long way.
  • Phone usage during a live show should be limited to the ADV Media Teams app for show purposes; everything else can wait. If you need to use your phone for reasons outside of ADV Media, please let your Production Manager or Director know.

With the Crew

This team works because of the people on it. We genuinely want everyone here to feel respected and supported, and that starts with how we treat each other.

  • Extend the same warmth and professionalism to your crew members that you would to a client.
  • If someone needs a hand and you have one free, offer it.
  • If something is bothering you about how a show went or how something was handled, bring it to leadership privately: the floor during a show is not the place for those conversations.
  • We are better together than we are separately, and that shows in the work.

With Venue Staff and Campus Personnel

Every campus and venue we work in has its own staff, its own rules, and its own culture. Part of showing up well means respecting that.

  • Introduce yourself warmly and let them know you are with ADV Media.
  • Follow all venue protocols and access procedures without pushback — they are doing their job just like we are doing ours.
  • Your digital ID card in the ADV Media Teams app is your on-site credential if anyone needs to verify your authorization.

Wrapping Up

The end of a show is just as important as the beginning. Strike is a team effort and everyone pitches in regardless of position. Before you head out, submit your equipment report and invoice through the ADV Media Teams app within 4 hours of a show or game ending to ensure prompt payment.

A Final Note

We are in the business of making moments matter. The couple whose day runs perfectly, the school that pulls off something they are proud of, the client who calls us back every single time; that is what we are here for and you are a big part of why it happens.

Thank you for being part of this team.
